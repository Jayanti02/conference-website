About the Conference

The Innovation & IP 360 International Conference on IPR and Emerging Frontiers is a global platform bringing together policymakers, researchers, academicians, industry leaders, and innovators. The conference focuses on intellectual property rights, disruptive technologies, and strategies that foster innovation and sustainable growth.

Conference Themes & Agenda

Day 1: The Evolving Landscape of IPR

  • International IP frameworks and emerging policies
  • Digital rights, NFTs, and metaverse IP challenges
  • Cross-border patent enforcement

Day 2: Technology, AI, and Innovation in IPR

  • AI and patentability of machine-generated inventions
  • IP in biotechnology and healthcare
  • Blockchain in IP enforcement

Day 3: Commercialization & Future Readiness

  • IP valuation, monetization, and licensing
  • University–industry collaborations
  • Sustainability and green technology IP

Call for Papers & Posters

The conference invites original research papers, case studies, posters, and startup innovations in all areas of IPR and emerging technologies. Selected papers will be published in Scopus-indexed proceedings.
